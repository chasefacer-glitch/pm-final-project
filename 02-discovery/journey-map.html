StreamLine Spotlight · Future-state journey

From a 20-minute scroll to a trusted pick tonight

Strategy: restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.

PersonaA longtime StreamLine subscriber who opens the app most evenings.
GoalFind a movie she's excited to watch tonight without spending 20 minutes scrolling.
Today's frictionNone of the recommendations stand out. After 20 minutes of scrolling, she closes the app and puts on a DVD.
0:000:30Under 2 minNext evening
Stage 1

Arrive

Tonight's Spotlight

User actionOpens app to one hand-picked film with curator note → zero scrolling required.
Internal stateBraced for a slog, then relieved. Someone already filtered for her.
Pain point addressed"None of the recommendations stand out." Human pick replaces generic algorithm rows.
Stage 2

Choose

Mood-based collections

User actionBrowses a 10-title "Quiet Sunday" collection → small, intentional set of choices.
Internal stateCurious, not anxious. The choice feels manageable.
Pain point addressedChoice overload from 15,000 titles and no way to browse by mood.
Stage 3

Watch

Commit and play

User actionPresses play within about two minutes → evening starts on StreamLine, not the shelf.
Internal stateExcited and confident she picked well.
Pain point addressedThe 20-minute scroll ending in app abandonment and a DVD.
Stage 4

Return

Weekly drop

User actionNext evening, opens a fresh curated drop shaped by what she loved → a reason to come back.
Internal stateTrusting. She expects something good waiting.
Pain point addressedEroding trust in the algorithm and the slide toward cancellation.

Competitive advantages over the DVD workaround

  1. Fresh discovery, not a fixed shelfCurated new picks → novelty without the risk of a bad choice.
  2. About 2 minutes, not 20One trusted pick → her evening goes to watching, not searching.
  3. It gets better over timeEvery watched pick teaches the curators → the DVD shelf never improves.

Timings (0:30, under 2 min, weekly drop) are design targets, not research findings. Source research: UXR-01, UXR-03, UXR-04, UXR-09, UXR-12; BUG-1091.
