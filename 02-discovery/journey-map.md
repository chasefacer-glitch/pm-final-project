# StreamLine Spotlight: Future-State Journey

**From a 20-minute scroll to a trusted pick tonight**

**Strategy:** Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.

## Persona

- **Persona:** A longtime StreamLine subscriber who opens the app most evenings.
- **Goal:** Find a movie she's excited to watch tonight without spending 20 minutes scrolling.
- **Today's friction:** None of the recommendations stand out. After 20 minutes of scrolling, she closes the app and puts on a DVD.

## Timeline

```
0:00            0:30               Under 2 min         Next evening
[1. Arrive] --> [2. Choose] -----> [3. Watch] -------> [4. Return]
 Relieved        Curious            Excited             Trusting
```

## The 4 Stages

### 1. Arrive: Tonight's Spotlight
- **User action:** Opens app to one hand-picked film with curator note → zero scrolling required.
- **Internal state:** Braced for a slog, then relieved. Someone already filtered for her.
- **Pain point addressed:** "None of the recommendations stand out." Human pick replaces generic algorithm rows.

### 2. Choose: Mood-based collections
- **User action:** Browses a 10-title "Quiet Sunday" collection → small, intentional set of choices.
- **Internal state:** Curious, not anxious. The choice feels manageable.
- **Pain point addressed:** Choice overload from 15,000 titles and no way to browse by mood.

### 3. Watch: Commit and play
- **User action:** Presses play within about two minutes → evening starts on StreamLine, not the shelf.
- **Internal state:** Excited and confident she picked well.
- **Pain point addressed:** The 20-minute scroll ending in app abandonment and a DVD.

### 4. Return: Weekly drop
- **User action:** Next evening, opens a fresh curated drop shaped by what she loved → a reason to come back.
- **Internal state:** Trusting. She expects something good waiting.
- **Pain point addressed:** Eroding trust in the algorithm and the slide toward cancellation.

## Competitive Advantages over the DVD Workaround

1. **Fresh discovery, not a fixed shelf:** Curated new picks → novelty without the risk of a bad choice.
2. **About 2 minutes, not 20:** One trusted pick → her evening goes to watching, not searching.
3. **It gets better over time:** Every watched pick teaches the curators → the DVD shelf never improves.

---

*Timings (0:30, under 2 min, weekly drop) are design targets, not research findings. Source research: UXR-01, UXR-03, UXR-04, UXR-09, UXR-12; BUG-1091.*
