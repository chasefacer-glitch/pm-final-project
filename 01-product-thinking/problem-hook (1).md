# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine Spotlight
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, subscribers will leave for streaming services that make it easier to discover great films.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user tired of scrolling the platform for a film, gives up, and opens up Netflix to find better suggestions.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We need to make it easier for film lovers to find movies they want to watch so they’re less likely to cancel and switch to another service.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Spotlight helps film lovers quickly find movies that match their tastes through handpicked collections within StreamLine. It’s urgent because more subscribers are canceling and competitors with curated catalogs are gaining momentum.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Yes. Engagement has stalled and more subscribers are canceling. That puts subscription revenue at risk and makes it worth testing Spotlight.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** Systemic. Viewers struggle to find movies they want to watch and may look elsewhere for recommendations. Flat engagement and rising subscriber churn suggest this could be a broader problem worth investigating.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** Yes. Spotlight gives viewers a smaller, handpicked selection to browse, making it easier to choose a movie without leaving StreamLine to look for recommendations.
