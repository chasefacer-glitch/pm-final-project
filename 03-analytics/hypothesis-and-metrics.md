## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** UXR-01: Priya scrolled for about 20 minutes, found nothing and put on a DVD. UXR-04: a lapsed subscriber called the catalog "a warehouse" and left for a curated competitor.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Only 29% of visitors press play. Spotlight users keep 74–79% at month one vs 63–68% for the full catalog. Wanderers churn 22 pts less with Spotlight.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Wanderers: subscribers who open the app now and then, browse trending picks and often leave without watching.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Too many titles and no sign of what's good, so Wanderers give up before pressing play.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** More Wanderers find something and press play in their first month, so fewer cancel and revenue is protected.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Month-one retention for new Wanderers who see Spotlight vs a test group that doesn't.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Power User sessions per week (4.8). Can't drop more than 5%.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 60 days (two monthly groups). Check play rate at week 3 and stop early if it hasn't moved.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on users giving up after 20 minutes of scrolling (UXR-01) and only 29% of visitors pressing play, I believe that giving Wanderers a small, handpicked Spotlight selection will get more of them watching and keep them subscribed, as measured by a 10-point gain in month-one retention vs a test group. I will protect Power User sessions per week and make a go/no-go decision after 60 days.
