# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Frustrated Viewer / A longtime StreamLine subscriber who wants to quickly find a movie she's excited to watch.
- **Primary metric:** Browse-to-play conversion rate.
- **Moment of misery:** None of the recommendations stand out. After 20 minutes of scrolling, she closes the app and puts on a DVD.
- **Guardrail:** Movie completion rate must not decrease.

## Scoring
| Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Spotlight Curated Rail | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | Reduces choice overload through human-led curation. |
| A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | Helps users make faster, more confident decisions. |
| A3 Hidden Gem Badge | 2 | 1 | Fill-In | Next | Highlights overlooked movies with minimal effort. |
| A4 Mood-Based Entry Point | 4 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | Matches how users choose movies based on mood. |
| A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue | 3 | 4 | Time Sinker | Later | Users currently lack trust in algorithmic recommendations. |
| A6 Spotlight Digest Email | 2 | 2 | Fill-In | Later | Our persona already opens the app regularly. |
| A7 Curator Profiles | 1 | 3 | Time Sinker | Later | Could build trust but doesn't directly solve discovery friction. |
| A8 Watch Party (Spotlight) | 1 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | Doesn't address the core discovery problem. |
| A9 Advanced Filter Engine | 2 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | Adds complexity and potentially more decision fatigue. |
| A10 Offline Download (Spotlight) | 1 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | Doesn't help users decide what to watch. |

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Reduces choice overload through human-led curation.
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Helps users make faster, more confident decisions.
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Matches how users choose movies based on mood.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Highlights overlooked movies with minimal effort.

### LATER, backlog
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, Users currently lack trust in algorithmic recommendations.
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Our persona already opens the app regularly.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Could build trust but doesn't directly solve discovery friction.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Doesn't address the core discovery problem.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Adds complexity and potentially more decision fatigue.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Doesn't help users decide what to watch.
